Usability test · minimum 5 vendors

The instrument is ready. No consented session is stored with this page until someone finishes it on a phone.

  1. Open the vendor prototype with study mode.
  2. Task success: update the oil log, save a strip reading, open yesterday’s reading.
  3. Completion time: each task is timed from the moment it is shown.
  4. System Usability Scale: ten questions, scored 0 to 100, immediately after.

Usability review has not reached 5 consented vendors.

Practice sessions stay on the phone and are not averaged. A mean is shown only after 5 sessions marked as consented vendors. That target is not a completed sample.

Limitations

  • Published curves come from potato-chip frying and demonstrate a method, not street-vendor oil life.
  • A phone reading of a strip is not a laboratory TPC measurement. Camera-to-human agreement is a different question from strip-to-laboratory TPC.
  • The 25% line is a research benchmark, not Philippine law.
  • The prototype does not detect pathogens, diagnose illness, or make a regulatory ruling.
  • Area summaries stay hidden below 5 consented stalls.

Future reference-TPC pathway

  1. A partner laboratory measures TPC on oils that consenting stalls are actually using.
  2. The same oils are measured again over time, with the strip photograph and the oil log beside each laboratory number.
  3. Phone bands are compared with the human band, and both are compared with the laboratory TPC. Those are two separate results.
  4. Until that local comparison is adequate, the vendor phone does not show hours remaining or a safety verdict.

This gate is blocked. Recording a single laboratory number in the API does not turn the countdown on.
