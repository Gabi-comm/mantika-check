Limitations

Published curves come from potato chip frying with an unspecified oil and demonstrate a method, not street vendor oil life. Phone strip readings are not direct TPC measurements; laboratory calibration remains future work. The 25% TPC line is a research benchmark, not Philippine law. The pilot does not detect pathogens or make regulatory rulings.
