Limitations

  • Published curves show a method, not vendor oil life
  • Phone to human agreement is not TPC accuracy
  • 25% TPC is a research benchmark, not Philippine law
  • Pilot summaries hidden below 5 stalls

Deployment gates

  1. Verify forecast errors on held out data
  2. Confirm a lab method for calibration
  3. No vendor countdown without local TPC data
  4. Consent and pseudonymised records
