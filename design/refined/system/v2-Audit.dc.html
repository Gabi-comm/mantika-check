01 · Audit of v1 moodboard

Keep the idea. Rebuild the execution.

Calibrated Paper was the right direction. Its weaknesses were in craft: identity, type rhythm, density and a few broken layouts.

Keep

  • Ink, paper, green, gold palette and their single meanings
  • Solid measured, dashed experimental, hatched uncertainty
  • Plex Sans and Plex Mono for text and values
  • Status as icon plus word plus border style
  • Research notices on every reading

Improve

  • Logo: a drawn wordmark replaces font text plus icon
  • One type scale shared by mobile and web, 4px rhythm
  • Web data text up from 12 to 14px, table rows 44px
  • Separate navigation, analysis and methodology regions
  • Scanner: real camera layout with honest feedback

Remove

  • Strip symbol: read as a battery at small sizes
  • Bricolage display face: a third family with no job
  • Wide tracked mono CHECK and uppercase eyebrows
  • Decorative ruled boxes and placeholder photo tiles
  • Hour timer as a hero number on vendor screens
