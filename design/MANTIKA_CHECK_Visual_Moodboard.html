Know what you're looking at

hue ≈ 40 to 70°

B · Blue

hue ≈ 190 to 240°

G · Green

hue ≈ 90 to 160°

Hue ranges are illustrative placeholders; the shipped guide uses the pipeline's configured thresholds.

What do RGB, HSV and CIELAB mean?

RGB is how the camera stores colour. HSV separates hue (which colour) from brightness. CIELAB is built to match human perception: L* is lightness, a* runs green to red, b* runs blue to yellow.

How to photograph a strip

Flat, plain background, daylight, card filling most of the frame, no flash.

Scientific limitations

Printed ink does not react to oil. This prototype validates the image pipeline only; small sample, one capture session, no chemical ground truth.
